Helping Women Build Healthier Lives, Stronger Confidence & Greater Financial Freedom

Hello and Welcome. I am Susan Assy Tumuhairwe, a CPD-Certified Nutrition and Wellness Coach, entrepreneur, and speaker. I am passionate about helping people live healthier, more fulfilling lives.

Through Nourish & Thrive by Susan, I empower individuals, particularly women, to make informed health choices, build sustainable wellness habits, and take charge of their wellbeing through practical nutrition and lifestyle guidance.

Through Women Prosper, I inspire and empower women to explore entrepreneurship, develop their potential, and pursue financial independence.

As a speaker, I share practical insights on nutrition and wellness, personal development, women’s empowerment, and entrepreneurship, inspiring individuals and communities to take positive steps towards lasting change.

My mission is simple: to educate, empower, and inspire people to nourish their health, grow their potential, and thrive in life.
